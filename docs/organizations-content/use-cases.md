# Coordination patterns for organizations

**Route:** `/enterprise/use-cases`

## Hero

**H1:** Coordination patterns for organizations

Start with the people who need to coordinate, the claims they need to verify,
the information they should control, and the choices they need if a provider
fails. Then decide whether Ethereum adds something a conventional system cannot.

## Digital asset systems

Create or manage digital representations of money, claims, or assets. Evaluate
issuance, redemption, transfer rules, custody, and the rights a token represents.

**CTA:** Designing digital asset systems

## Onchain market infrastructure

Use open protocols for settlement, collateral, lending, exchange, or market
coordination. Evaluate every protocol, asset, and data source as part of the
system’s risk model.

**CTA:** Evaluating onchain market infrastructure

## Verifiable credentials

Issue and verify claims with less reliance on a central database. Evaluate
revocation, portability, selective disclosure, and the rights of the people
whose information is involved.

**CTA:** Designing verifiable credential systems

## Shared records and provenance

Use a shared, tamper-evident record where multiple parties need to verify a
history without one party controlling the database. A record can prove that data
has not changed; it cannot prove that the data was true when entered.

## Questions to ask in every case

Who participates? Which rules matter? What information is public or private?
Who can change the system? Can users and organizations recover or exit without
the operator’s cooperation?
