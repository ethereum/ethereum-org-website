# Diagrams

The developer docs explain protocol mechanics with schematic PNGs (adapted from
_Ethereum EVM illustrated_). PNGs don't follow the theme, can't be translated,
and give screen readers one alt string. This set of primitives replaces them
one diagram at a time with theme-aware, translatable, accessible React figures.
`gas-tx.png` (`GasTx`) is the first and `gas.png` (`GasEvm`) the second; `.claude/skills/devdocs-diagrams/SKILL.md`
has the recipe and the list of the PNGs still to convert.

`GasTx` itself is drawn as a simple isometric "anatomy" figure (a fuel rig,
`GasTx/gas-rig.tsx`), built from the shared anatomy primitives in
`Diagrams/anatomy/`: `isoProjection`, `IsoPlate`, `IsoSolid`, `IsoCylinder`,
`IsoPipe`, `AnatomyLabels` and `DiagramStage`, coloured by the `--diagram-*`
tokens (defined in `src/styles/semantic-tokens.css`, registered as Tailwind
colours in `theme.css`, read through `anatomy/tokens.ts`). It is lazy-loaded
with `next/dynamic`.

## Rules for every diagram

Later diagrams must follow these. The shared pieces live in
`src/components/Diagrams/anatomy/` (story: Components / Data Viz / Diagrams /
Anatomy).

- **No vanishing lines.** Colours come only from the `--diagram-*` tokens in
  `src/styles` (`--diagram-bg`, `-plate`, `-edge`, `-pipe`, `-entity`, `-gas`,
  `-revert`), used through static literal classes (`stroke-diagram-pipe`,
  never a string-built class) or `DIAGRAM_COLOR` values that carry a
  fallback. The drawing's root `<svg>` spreads `DIAGRAM_SVG_FALLBACKS`
  (`fill="none" stroke="currentColor"`), so a missing utility degrades to a
  neutral line instead of nothing or black. Shapes that must not be stroked
  say so (`stroke="none"` or `stroke-none`).
- **Guard.** `tests/e2e/diagram-strokes.spec.ts` walks every shape of every
  `DiagramStage` on the pages it lists, for every preset and step, in light
  and dark, and fails if a shape that should be stroked has no visible stroke
  or any fill is black. Add each new diagram's page to it, and give the
  next-step button `data-diagram-next`.
- **Nothing black, nothing unresolved.** Every fill and stroke resolves to a
  site token in both themes. An unset SVG fill renders black, so set one on
  every shape (even clip-path geometry). Audit with a script, not by eye.
- **Shading formula.** A solid's three faces are tints of one hue mixed with
  the background token: `color-mix(in oklab, var(<hue>) N%, var(--background))`
  with N = 15% (top), 30% (inline-start face), 45% (inline-end face). Mixing
  with the dark background makes the same formula adapt to dark mode.
  `anatomyFill(hue, face)` implements it.
- **Plate (world state).** `--background-highlight` fill, a thin
  `--border-high-contrast` edge, side faces a slightly darker tint of that
  neutral (25% / 40%). Never black.
- **Colour roles.** Blue (`accent-a`) = accounts and contracts. Orange
  (`diagram-state`; `yellow-700` light, `yellow-600` dark, a true orange,
  not gold) = gas and value: tanks, levels, flowing gas, glow, meters.
  Neutral = structure: plate (`border-high-contrast` edge), pipes at rest
  (`body-medium`), leader lines. The failure state uses `diagram-revert`.
- **Active state.** The part to watch takes its role colour: stronger tints
  and a role-coloured hairline (blue for accounts and contracts, orange for
  gas), and its label and leader switch to that colour. Never the site
  `primary`, never a heavier stroke.
- **Strokes.** Hairlines (0.6px, non-scaling), hierarchy by colour. Pipes
  are thin tubes: a 1.4px `body-medium` stroke with a 0.4px plate-coloured
  highlight. A one-shot orange dash flows along a pipe only while gas moves
  through it.
- **Show the quantity moving.** Levels change with the step (a tank fills,
  drains as gas burns, drains the rest back), a burning part glows orange in
  proportion (`IsoSolid glow`: top face and outline tinted toward the gas
  hue alone, never blended with blue), and readouts count up in step with the
  drawing (`useCountUp`, the one JS-driven motion: a one-shot ~700ms tween,
  instant under reduced motion).
- **Simple parts.** One simple solid per object, at most one icon per object,
  each object on its own footprint (no overlaps).
- **Labels.** Concept names only, single line, 12px, fully outside the plate
  (including its sloping front edge across the label's whole width), each
  with a short straight (or single-elbow) leader that never crosses a pipe, a
  solid or another label. Labels are focusable: pointing at or focusing one
  highlights its part and shows its definition in the status line (no
  tooltip).
- Hairline outlines (0.6px) with hierarchy by colour: `--diagram-line-faint`
  (seams), `--diagram-line` (outlines), `--diagram-line-strong` (hovered).
  Light tints at rest, full role colour only on the active or hovered part.
- Static detail (`isoDetail`) and soft gradient shadows (`isoShadow`, no
  filters or blur) are built once at module level and never re-render.
- Animate only opacity, transform and stroke-dashoffset; runs and
  animations pause off screen.
  `AnatomyLabels` drops any label that would leave the drawing, touch any
  shape (the plate included) or touch another label or leader, earlier spots
  winning, so narrow screens fall back to fewer labels plus the figcaption.
- **One shell: `InteractiveDiagram`** (`src/components/Diagrams/interactive-diagram.tsx`).
  Every interactive diagram uses it, so controls sit in the same places
  everywhere. It wraps `DiagramFigure` (intro text as the figcaption, above)
  and `DiagramStage` (one card). From a 640px card width (container query)
  the illustration takes the remaining width and a 15rem panel sits on the
  inline-end side, vertically centred; narrower, the panel sits directly
  under the illustration, same order. Slots, top to bottom:
  1. `presets`: `DiagramPresets`, "Example values" as a segmented control
     (vertical full-width rows in the side panel, a row when stacked).
  2. `transport`: `DiagramTransport`, the primary action (full width in the
     side panel; "Send transaction", "Run", ...), then previous, "Step x of
     n", next, reset as 32px icon buttons. Same icons and order everywhere;
     next carries `data-diagram-next` for the stroke guard.
  3. `stepCaption`: `DiagramCaption`, a polite live region sized to its
     longest step (pass every caption), so nothing jumps.
  4. `readout`: `DiagramReadout`, a compact two-column list with right-aligned
     tabular mono numbers.
- **Dark mode.** The face tints are tokens: `--diagram-mix-top/-start/-end`
  are 7/14/22% in light and 20/32/45% in dark at rest, and
  `--diagram-mix-*-active` 15/30/45% and 30/50/70% for the active or hovered
  part, so blue and orange still read as colour. Dark uses one step lighter
  hues (`blue-300`, `yellow-500`).
- **Verify.** Screenshot light and dark at 1440px, 1024px and 390px (2x),
  with reduced motion and RTL, and each step of each preset. Check no black
  paint, no label collisions, no labels on shapes or the plate, no leader
  crossings, nothing clipped. Translated pages keep the old image until the
  intl pipeline runs, so check RTL with `dir="rtl"` or the `ar` story.

## Tokens

Every colour is a semantic token; there are no hex values in components. Five
`diagram-*` tokens were added (following the `staking-*` precedent) because no
existing pair passed AA in both themes. Contrast is text 4.5:1, strokes and
icons 3:1.

## Primitives

- `DiagramFigure`: `figure` + screen-reader `description` (`aria-describedby`) +
  visible `caption`. Every diagram starts here.
- `DiagramNode`: `tone` (entity, state, chip), `shape` (box, circle, pill),
  `layout` (stack, inline), optional `icon`, `definition` (Tooltip), children.
- `DiagramGroup`: `variant` (outlined, dashed), `tone` (state, entity), `title`.
- `DiagramConnector`: `orientation` (inline, block, responsive), `line`
  (solid, dashed), `arrow` (end, start, both, none), `label`, `labelPlacement`,
  `lineClassName` and `tokenClassName` animation hooks.
- `DiagramElbow`: `from` (top, bottom), `to` (start, end), static only.
- `DiagramBadge`: `border` (dashed, solid), `tone`, `icon`, `label`,
  `definition`, `level` (gauge, a number or `"inherit"`).
- `DiagramPill` (`shape` pill, tab; `tone` neutral, revert) and `DiagramTitle`.

Layout is HTML in CSS grid: nodes in columns, connectors in their own lanes
between them. Text wraps instead of clipping, grid columns follow `dir` so RTL
mirrors for free, and nothing is measured in JS.

## Motion

- CSS only, on SVG and HTML: `stroke-dashoffset` flow, a travelling dot
  (`transform`), opacity, and the badge gauge (`scaleY`). No Motion library,
  canvas, WebGL or JS animation loop. A diagram's client island only flips
  `data-*` attributes on a `group/diagram` wrapper.
- 500-700 ms per step, ease-out, one-shot (`animate-diagram-*` in `theme.css`).
  No idle or looping animation.
- One thing moves at a time, and it is the only element in `primary`.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing moves: each step shows its end state,
  and the controls still work.

## Accessibility

- `figure` + `figcaption`, plus a `description` that narrates the whole flow.
- Nodes and badges with a `definition` are focusable and show it on hover and
  focus. Containers expose only their label as the trigger.
- Controls are design-system `Button`s in a `ButtonGroup`, with `aria-pressed`
  on presets and labelled icon buttons for previous/next.
- Step changes are announced in a polite `role="status"` caption.
- Labels never use `whitespace-nowrap`; German-length strings wrap.

## How to convert the next PNG

1. **Read the PNG** and list its nodes, groups, connectors, pills and badges.
   Map each one to a primitive. If something doesn't fit, add a variant to a
   primitive rather than a new component.
2. **Write the strings** in `src/intl/en/component-diagrams.json` under a
   `<diagram>-*` prefix: labels, one-line definitions, a caption, and a
   `description` that narrates the whole drawing.
3. **Build the Server Component** in `src/components/Diagrams/<Name>/index.tsx`:
   `DiagramFigure` > grid of nodes and connector lanes. Add a small
   `"use client"` island only if the diagram is interactive, with a plain-TS
   model that everything reads from.
4. **Wire it into content.** Register it in `docsComponents`
   (`src/layouts/Docs.tsx`) and swap the image line in the English markdown
   only. Leave the PNG and translated markdown alone.
5. **Add stories** (each state, dark, `ar`, mobile, reduced motion), check them
   at 2x in both themes and at 360 px, then run `pnpm lint`, `pnpm type-check`
   and `pnpm build-storybook`.
