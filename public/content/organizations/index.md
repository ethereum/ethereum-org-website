Ethereum is open infrastructure for people and organizations that need to coordinate without giving one operator permanent control over the system.

Whether you are evaluating a product, a public service, or a new venture, start with the problem, understand the trade-offs, and choose the path that fits.

## Start with a real question {#start-with-a-real-question}

Ethereum can be useful when several parties need to share a record, settle value, verify a claim, or coordinate without agreeing on one party to own the infrastructure. If one trusted organization can run the process alone, a conventional system may be simpler.

## What this hub is for {#what-this-hub-is-for}

These paths are educational starting points, not a vendor directory or an endorsement of a particular product, provider, or deployment. They explain the choices that determine whether a system remains open, secure, private, and workable for the people who rely on it.
