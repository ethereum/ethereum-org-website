## Shared records and provenance {#shared-records-and-provenance}

Use a shared, tamper-evident record where multiple parties need to verify a history without one party controlling the database. A record can prove that data has not changed; it cannot prove that the data was true when entered.

## Questions to ask in every case {#questions-to-ask-in-every-case}

Who participates? Which rules matter? What information is public or private? Who can change the system? Can users and organizations recover or exit without the operator’s cooperation?
