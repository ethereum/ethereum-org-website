Ethereum is open infrastructure. There is no sales process or partner portal required to begin learning and testing. Start small, use public documentation, and retain the ability to change providers.

## Learn the stack {#learn-the-stack}

Start with Ethereum developer documentation, smart-contract fundamentals, layer 2 learning material, and the Ethereum roadmap. Use test networks and small, reversible experiments before a production commitment.

## Standards you may meet {#standards-you-may-meet}

Use established standards where they fit, and check a standard’s status before relying on it. A draft standard may change. Publish interfaces and assumptions that a counterparty needs to verify or replace your system.

## Assessing external help {#assessing-external-help}

ethereum.org does not rank providers. When assessing a custodian, node provider, rollup framework, auditor, or integrator, ask whether code and interfaces are open, whether an exit path works, and whether switching costs are visible.
