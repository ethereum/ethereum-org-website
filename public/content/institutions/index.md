Public agencies, multilateral organizations, civil-society groups, and NGOs can use Ethereum to explore durable infrastructure for coordination, records, payments, and credentials. The question is whether a shared public layer gives people and institutions more independence, verifiability, and control than a single operator can.

## A public-interest standard {#a-public-interest-standard}

Technology does not make a service legitimate by itself. Public-interest deployment should protect privacy, accessibility, agency, and recourse; be understandable enough to govern; and remain usable if an integrator, provider, or funder changes course.
